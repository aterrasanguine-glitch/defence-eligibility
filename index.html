How eligibility is calculated

1. The calculator compares your date of birth against inclusive start/end dates entered from a specific UPSC notification.

2. It checks the education, gender and marital conditions saved for that particular route.

3. Each academy is assessed separately. Being eligible for one does not imply eligibility for another.

4. Future opportunity count includes only future exam cycles with rules you have entered and marked official.

Important limitationsThis is a screening tool, not an eligibility certificate. Nationality, medical/physical standards, detailed degree subjects, final-year documentation, marital exceptions, age relaxations and other notification-specific clauses may require manual review. A "pass" means only that the configured fields passed.
